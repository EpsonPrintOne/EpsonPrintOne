## The Deadline That Started It All

Marcus is a graduate student at NTU, splitting his time between coursework, research, and a part-time teaching assistantship — all of which seem to demand printed slides, papers, and marked scripts at the worst possible moments.

When his old printer finally gave up — the third jammed cartridge in a month, right before a batch of scripts was due — he braced himself for the usual choice: spend several hundred dollars on a new printer he couldn't really justify on a student budget, or keep trekking to the campus print shop every time something was due.

## No Upfront Cost, Predictable Monthly Bills

A labmate mentioned PrintOne instead. No upfront payment for the printer itself — just a flat monthly subscription that fit easily into what he was already budgeting each semester. He picked **PrintOne Pro**, and a few days later an Epson EcoTank was delivered and set up in his room, ready to go. One predictable bill each month, instead of a big purchase he'd have had to save up for.

## Two Years, Zero Printer Worries

The plan runs on a straightforward two-year subscription — and ink, servicing, and support are all part of it. When the paper feed started acting up before finals, a technician came by and fixed it on the spot, no extra charge, no scrambling for a repair shop between lectures. With two years of free delivery, installation and onsite servicing covered upfront, printer trouble simply stopped being something he had to plan around.

## More Time for Research, Less Time on Repairs

That's turned out to be the real value for Marcus — not just the predictable monthly cost instead of a big purchase, but the mental space it freed up. He spends less time worrying about ink levels and warranty terms, and more time on his research, his TA work, and catching up with friends over dinner before the next deadline hits. One less thing to manage, in a schedule that's already full.
