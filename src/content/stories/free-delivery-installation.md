A printer is supposed to help you get things done. Getting it into your home or office shouldn't become another project on your to-do list.

Yet when you're comparing printers, it's easy to focus on the price tag and forget the next question: what happens after you order?

Who brings it over? Who handles installation? And how much of your own time will you need to set aside before you can get back to the work you bought it for?

With the PrintOne program <i class="text-primary dark:text-darkmode-primary">in partnership with Epson</i>, **free standard delivery and installation are included in your subscription**. Print-IQ delivers and installs your Epson printer, so you're choosing a service — not simply a box to unpack.

## Your time belongs to your work, not your printer setup

Perhaps you need a printer for school worksheets. Perhaps you're preparing coursework, handling customer documents or getting a small office organised.

In each case, the printer is a means to an end. What you actually want is to get on with the task.

Buying outright can still suit someone who prefers to own and manage their equipment from the start. Some retailers also offer delivery or installation. The useful comparison is therefore not just "Which printer costs less?" but "What does this particular offer include, and what will I have to arrange myself?"

PrintOne makes delivery and standard installation part of the plan from the outset.
